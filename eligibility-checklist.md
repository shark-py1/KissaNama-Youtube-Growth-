# YouTube Monetization Readiness Checklist

Check the **Earn** tab in YouTube Studio for the current country-specific requirements and your channel's live progress.

## Channel foundations
- [ ] Channel has a clear identity and audience promise
- [ ] Two-step verification is enabled on the Google account
- [ ] Advanced features access is available where required
- [ ] No active Community Guidelines strikes blocking application
- [ ] Channel complies with YouTube monetization policies
- [ ] AdSense for YouTube setup is completed when prompted

## Content quality and originality
- [ ] Scripts are original or meaningfully transformed with substantial creative input
- [ ] Narration and editing add clear value
- [ ] Visuals, music, and sound effects are licensed, owned, or otherwise permitted
- [ ] No repetitive, mass-produced, or minimally changed reused content
- [ ] Historical and factual claims are checked; fiction is labelled appropriately
- [ ] Titles and thumbnails accurately represent the video

## Growth tracking
- [ ] Subscriber progress is recorded monthly
- [ ] Eligible public watch hours are tracked separately from general watch time
- [ ] Eligible Shorts views are tracked separately
- [ ] Current eligibility thresholds have been checked in YouTube Studio → Earn

**Reminder:** Subscriber, watch-hour, Shorts-view, and feature thresholds can change. Do not rely on old screenshots or third-party summaries; verify the current thresholds directly in YouTube Studio and official YouTube Help.
