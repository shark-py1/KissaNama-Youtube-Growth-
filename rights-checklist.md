# Copyright & Reused-Content Checklist

For every upload:

- [ ] Story script is original, public-domain, licensed, or used with appropriate rights
- [ ] Music licence permits YouTube use and monetization
- [ ] Sound-effect licence permits YouTube use and monetization
- [ ] Images, footage, and AI-generated assets comply with tool terms and applicable rights
- [ ] Any third-party clips are necessary, limited, and supported by a valid legal basis where applicable
- [ ] Sources and licence evidence are saved
- [ ] Video adds meaningful original narration, structure, commentary, or creative storytelling

A copyright claim and YouTube's reused/inauthentic-content monetization review are different issues. Permission to use an asset does not automatically guarantee monetization eligibility.
